# Coffee

A tiny example map — beans, ways to brew, and a few drinks.

## 1. Beans

The two species behind almost all coffee.

- **Arabica**: Smoother and sweeter with more aroma; most specialty coffee.
- **Robusta**: More caffeine and a bolder, more bitter cup; common in espresso blends.

## 2. Brewing

How hot water meets the grounds.

- **Espresso**: Hot water forced through fine grounds under pressure; concentrated.
- **Pour Over**: Water poured slowly over grounds in a filter; clean and bright.
- **French Press**: Grounds steeped, then pressed down; full-bodied.

## 3. Drinks

A few espresso-based classics.

- **Latte**: Espresso with lots of steamed milk and a little foam.
- **Cappuccino**: Roughly equal espresso, steamed milk, and foam.
- **Americano**: Espresso diluted with hot water.
